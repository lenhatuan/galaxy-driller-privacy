Galaxy Driller – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Galaxy Driller (package vn.tuan.galaxydriller, "the Game") handles information. The Game is published on Google Play by its developer ("we", "us"). The Game is designed for a general audience that includes children.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The Game has no account, login, chat or social features.

Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward.

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA / "tag for child-directed treatment") and as under the age of consent, and are limited to G-rated content.
  • The Game does not access the Android Advertising ID, and no personalized (interest-based) advertising or remarketing is used.
  • To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address, device type, operating system version, app version and ad interaction data. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are configured in line with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been collected, please contact us and we will help remove it.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third-party service in the Game is Google AdMob, as described above.

5. Data security and retention

Network traffic from the Game uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not retain any. Uninstalling the Game deletes all locally saved game data.

6. Your choices

You can play the Game without ever watching an ad. You can stop all processing at any time by uninstalling the Game.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the developer email address shown on the Game's Google Play Store listing.
